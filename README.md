## Rug Mosiac — Homepage Builder Prompt

**Brand in one line:** Handmade custom rugs, tufted to order in Kigali. Any design, any size, yours forever.

---

### Overall Aesthetic
Dark luxury. Background is near-black `#171513`. Cream and warm off-white text. Serif font for all headlines — elegant, not loud. Body text in a clean sans-serif. Zero clutter. Images do 90% of the work. The page should feel like a high-end boutique you stumbled onto, not a marketplace.

---

### Top Bar (thin strip above nav)
One single line of text, centered, small caps:
> *Handmade in Kigali · Made to order · Any design, yours forever*

---

### Navigation
Logo left: **RUG MOSIAC** in serif italic.
Links: `Catalogue` · `How It Works` · `Our Story` · `Contact`
Right side: one pill button — **Order on WhatsApp** in green.
Sticky on scroll. Transparent over hero, solid dark when scrolled.

---

### Hero Section
Full-bleed image — a stunning flat-lay or room shot of your best rug filling the entire screen. Subtle dark gradient overlay at the bottom. Over the image, bottom-left aligned text:

**Small badge:** `Made in Kigali · Since [year]`
**Big serif headline (2–3 lines):**
> *Floor art,*
> *made for you.*

**Two buttons below:** `Explore the catalogue →` and `Order on WhatsApp`

Auto-cycling between 3 hero images. No dots or arrows — just a slow crossfade.

---

### Shop by Category Strip
Section title: `Shop by style.`
Four equal image cards in a row (2×2 on mobile):
- **Sports** — photo of your Lakers or PSG rug
- **Cartoon** — Naruto or SpongeBob rug
- **Animals** — Kaboss or Luna rug
- **Custom** — a dramatic before/after or client photo

Each card: full image, name overlaid bottom-left, arrow `→`. Hover slightly lifts the card.

---

### How It Works (3 steps)
Minimal numbered list, no icons, just numbers and text:

**01 — Pick your design**
Browse the catalogue or bring your own idea — a photo, a logo, a vibe.

**02 — We craft it by hand**
Every rug is hand-tufted in wool. We'll confirm size, colours, and timeline before we start.

**03 — Delivered to your door**
Rugs shipped or delivered across Kigali. Most orders ready in 3–4 weeks.

---

### Featured Rugs (product grid)
Section title: `Made-to-order pieces.`
3 cards wide on desktop, 1 on mobile. Each card:
- Full square image
- Rug name in serif
- Small tag (Sports / Art / Custom etc.)
- `View →` link

No prices shown — instead a small line: *"Price on request"* or *"Order via WhatsApp"*

---

### Brand Story Block
Two images side by side — one of the tufting process, one of a finished rug in a real room. Right side has text:

**Small label:** `Our craft`
**Serif headline:** *Every rug is a one-of-a-kind.*
**Short paragraph:** Since [year], we've been turning ideas into floor art — hand-tufted in wool, built to last, designed to stop people in their tracks.
**Link:** `Read our story →`

---

### Reviews Strip
Star rating `★★★★★` — headline: `Our happy clients.`
3 short rotating customer quotes. Clean cards, no profile photos needed — just name and quote.

---

### Instagram / Portfolio Teaser
6-image grid pulled from or mimicking your best Instagram shots. Title: `Follow the work.`
Button: `@rugmosiac on Instagram →`

---

### Footer
Logo top center.
Four columns: `Shop` · `Company` · `Connect` · `Order`
Connect column: Instagram link + WhatsApp number.
Bottom line: `© 2025 Rug Mosiac · Kigali, Rwanda`

---

**Tech notes for the builder:**
- Font pairing: `Playfair Display` (serif headlines) + `DM Sans` (body)
- Primary bg: `#171513` · Text: `#f2ede6` · Accent: `#c97d3a`
- Mobile-first. Every section stacks to single column on phones.
- WhatsApp CTA appears in nav, hero, and floating bottom-right button on all pages.
- No cart, no checkout — all orders go through WhatsApp.
